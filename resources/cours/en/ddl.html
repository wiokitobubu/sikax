Degrees of freedom and mobility

Degrees of freedom of a body

In the plane, a free body has 3 degrees of freedom: two translations (x, y) and one rotation (θ). In space it has 6. Each joint removes some of them.

Mobility of a mechanism

The mobility m is the number of independent parameters needed to know the position of every body. It is also the number of motors needed to drive the mechanism.

Grübler's formula (plane): m = 3·(N − 1) − Σ (3 − dofi)

N: number of bodies, ground included. Example: slider-crank (4 bodies, 3 revolute joints, 1 prismatic): m = 3·3 − 4·2 = 1.

This formula assumes independent joints. Sikax computes the true mobility from the rank of the closure equations, which also detects internal mobilities and special configurations.
